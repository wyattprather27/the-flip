The original article opened with two recent, high-profile incidents in the same neighborhood over
one weekend and described the city as gripped by a "crime wave." The incidents were real and
serious. The trend line the phrase implies is not.

The city police department's own year-to-date data, which the original piece did not cite, shows
violent crime down 12% compared to the same period last year — the third consecutive year of
decline, following a spike in 2022–2023. Two incidents in one weekend can happen during a
multi-year decline; monthly crime counts are naturally noisy, and a bad weekend is not the same
thing as a reversal in the trend.

This isn't a claim that crime is a non-issue or that the two incidents don't deserve coverage. It's
that "wave" is a trend word, and the trend — measured over any window longer than a weekend — is
running the opposite direction.
